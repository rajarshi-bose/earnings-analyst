      How this reads a release

      The release is the press release a company files with the SEC on Form 8-K (item 2.02, usually exhibit 99.1). Financial tables, the forward-looking statements notice and other legal boilerplate are left out, so the score reflects the words management chose to describe the quarter.

      Each word is scored from 1 to 5 for how strongly positive or negative it reads in an earnings context, using a word list written for financial language: "loss" and "headwinds" count as negative, "liability" and "tax" do not. A word right after "not", "no" or "without" is skipped. Net tone is positive weight minus negative weight over the total, from -100 to +100.

      Prices are daily closes adjusted for splits and dividends. Quarters follow the company's own reporting dates where the SEC has them.
